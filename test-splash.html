Option 2 - Test Installed PWA:

  3. Launch the app from your home screen/desktop

  • Browser: Splash shows every time you visit splash.html
  • Installed PWA: Splash shows when launching the app

  • Uninstall & Reinstall: If already installed, uninstall and reinstall to see changes

Customize Splash Duration:

Edit splash.html and change this line:
setTimeout(function() { ... }, 2000);
Change 2000 to any milliseconds (1000 = 1 second)
