📋 How to Use:

  1. Upload Logo: Click the button below to select your main logo (main aap logo.png from vendor/img/completions/)
  2. Preview: All icon sizes will be generated automatically with maroon background
  3. Download: Click individual download buttons or "Download All Icons"
  4. Replace: Save the downloaded icons to vendor/img/icons/ folder
  5. Done: Your PWA will now use the new icons with maroon background!
